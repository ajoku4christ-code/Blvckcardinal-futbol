Partner enquiry

Questions, answered.

Who can enter a team?

Grassroots teams from Lagos can register. Squad limits, age rules and eligibility are published in the tournament rules when registration opens.

How does a team qualify?

Registered teams play qualifiers. The teams that qualify enter the draw and the group stage in September.

Is there an entry fee?

Registration fees, if any, are announced when registration opens. Nothing is collected through this website.

Where is the Grand Final held?

The venue is announced with the confirmed schedule. It will host the final on October 1st.

Can players under 18 take part?

Yes, with a parent or guardian's permission. Players under 18 are covered by our safeguarding rules in the tournament handbook.

How do players get noticed by clubs?

Matches are recorded and player profiles are shared with clubs and academies in our network. Introductions are never a guarantee of a trial or signing.

How do companies become partners?

Choose a package or a service partnership above and send an enquiry. We reply with the full proposal, benefits inventory and agreement.
